# 5.82.0

*Released 22 October 2026*

Crew Finder can now suggest a team for a call. Pick one call, press Plan..., choose a mix of new and experienced crew (or best rated, or most experienced) and any licence minimums, and THE GOAT ticks a suggested team in the results for you to check and send with Add or Add & Confirm. Admin only.

- **New and experienced:** new means under 144 hours in the current probation run, the same figure as the Hours tab and the Probation lane. Someone returning after an 18-month gap counts as new. A mix is written new first, so "80/20 new to old" means 80% new.
- **The plan counts who is already on the call,** so re-planning after declines rebalances the mix. On small calls the experienced share rounds up. If one group runs short, the places go to the other group and the plan says so.
- **Crew Boss calls** get one experienced person, preferring crew who have been on a Crew Boss call in the last 3 years.
- **Licences:** licences you tick in the Finder apply to everyone (expired cards never count); licence minimums like "2 with forklift" are set in the Plan form. If a call's notes mention a licence, the plan asks whether to add it.
- **Warnings:** crew with a fatigue, venue-change or 16-in-24 warning are never ticked; they're listed as "could fill with a warning". Crew without a venue induction, or holding a clashing open offer, are picked only after everyone else.
- **Ask the GOAT** can build the same plan ("plan call 38750, 80/20 new to old"). It reads the plan back first, and Open in Crew Finder shows it with the ticks in place. It never adds anyone itself.
- The first plan of the day takes 10-15 seconds while hours are worked out; later plans are quick.
