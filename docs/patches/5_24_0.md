# 5.24.0

*Released 2 September 2026*

Calls can now be cancelled without being deleted.

Open a call and choose **Stand down**. The call stays in its booking, everyone assigned to it moves to a new **Cancelled** status rather than being removed, and their calendars are cleared. Keeping the crew list is the point: it is the record of who was committed at the moment the customer pulled out, which is what a cancellation charge is argued from.

Standing a call down asks two things every time and defaults neither — whether the cancellation is chargeable, and why. Both are recorded against the call and shown on it afterwards.

A cancelled call drops out of the Schedule, the open-offer feeds, the times-outstanding lanes and the crew contact resolver. It stays visible in the booking, greyed and struck through, with a banner giving the reason, whether it is chargeable, and who cancelled it and when. Find Crew and Enter Times are removed from it — there is no crew to find and no times to enter.

If the call feeds other calls, you are warned and shown which ones. They are never cancelled for you.

**No notification reaches crew yet.** Crew Hub cannot receive cancellation notices until the portal side ships. After standing a call down, THE GOAT lists everyone who still needs telling along with their numbers, and says plainly that nothing has been sent. Ring them.

Also fixed: a cancelled crew assignment could previously be rewritten to "Declined" without warning when that person declined a different, linked call — destroying the cancellation record.
