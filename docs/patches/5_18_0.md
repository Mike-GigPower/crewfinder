# 5.18.0

*Released 25 August 2026*

A new dashboard answering how the business is tracking, rather than what happened last week.

Seven panels. Headline numbers now carry their direction against the same period last year, so a figure comes with a trend rather than sitting on its own. A waterfall breaks the revenue change into how much came from hours and how much from rates. A slope chart shows which customers moved and by how much. A scatter puts every major customer's hours against what they pay per hour, so the ones priced below the rest are visible rather than buried in a table.

The financial year view shows this year's cumulative hours and revenue against last year's full year, with work already booked continuing the line in its own colour, and a target set at 10% more hours and 13.5% more revenue. The target follows last year's seasonal shape rather than a straight line, because this business earns 7% of its year in the first eight weeks — a straight line would read as a disaster every winter.

Notes explaining how each figure is built are collapsed by default. Two things stay visible: that revenue is labour only and dated to when the work happened, and that no margin figure is available yet.
