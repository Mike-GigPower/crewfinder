# 5.7.1

*Released 19 August 2026*

Adds entry as a link type in Manage Inductions, alongside register, sitemap, info, parking and contact. Staff entry points and loading docks are distinct from precinct maps and are now classified as such rather than being folded in with them.

Groundwork for induction reference content moving from Crew Hub's database into the GOAT catalogue, so venue links are edited in one place.
