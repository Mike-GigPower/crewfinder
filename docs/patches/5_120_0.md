# 5.120.0

*Released 24 November 2026*

A booking's Calls tab now tells you at a glance which calls still want times.

**Green means the call is locked** — accounting has it and nothing more will change. **Lavender means the times have been accepted.** They are the same two colours SmartStaff's own booking page uses, so the two screens finally agree with each other.

Everything else gets a small label rather than a colour. **ALL IN — ACCEPT** when every confirmed crew member has times and the call is only waiting on your review. **TIMES 3/9** when some are in and some aren't. **NO TIMES 0/10** when none have arrived. **NO-SHOW TO CONFIRM** when a crew boss has reported somebody and Accept is held until you confirm or reject it. A call that hasn't run yet stays plain — it owes nothing.

**Every call row now has a ⏱ Times button**, in the booking dialog and in All Bookings, so you can go straight to the times without opening the call first. In All Bookings the words "⏱ Enter Times →" used to sit on the right of each row looking like a button while actually opening the call dialog. It is a real button now.

**The Times outstanding lane gives you three ways in.** The booking name opens the booking on its Calls tab, the venue and call name open the call, and ⏱ Times → opens the times grid. Clicking anywhere else on the card still opens the times, exactly as before.

**⏱ Enter Times is now ⏱ View / Enter Times** — most of the time you are going in to look, not to type, and the old name made it sound like a commitment.

Nothing about how times are entered, saved or accepted has changed. This release only makes the state easier to see and quicker to reach.
