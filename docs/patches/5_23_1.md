# 5.23.1

*Released 31 August 2026*

Fixes a bug where sending induction reminders to a large session reported
"Could not send: Sessions service unavailable" even though every email had
already been delivered.

THE GOAT was only waiting 20 seconds for the send to finish. Sending to more
than about 36 booked candidates takes longer than that — 56 people takes around
32 seconds — so the app stopped listening while the send was still running and
reported that silence as an outage. All four reminder runs on 31 August were
affected: 302 emails, every one delivered, every one reported to ops as a failure.

The app now waits up to three minutes for the two actions that email everyone
(Send reminders and Cancel session). If it ever does time out, it says the send
is still running rather than that it failed. Pressing the button again remains
safe — anyone already emailed is skipped.

No change to what the emails say, or to who receives them.
