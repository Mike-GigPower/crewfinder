# 5.96.3

*Released 9 November 2026*

Crew Finder Plan: a call that's already full now says so, and booking plans fold up so you can still see the candidates.

Planning a call with no places left now shows "Call is full — nothing to plan." instead of listing crew who could fill with a warning. On a full Crew Boss call the plan shows the places line instead of "Boss place left empty".

In a booking plan, each call now folds to one line showing its places, how many are short and how many crew could fill with a warning. Click a call to see its picks, or use Expand all. The candidate list below stays on screen.
