# 4.5.0

*Released 17 July 2026*

# THE GOAT v4.5.0 — Employment Hero tracking, in-app contract signing & the "All Docs Received" gate

*The biggest onboarding release yet — the path from applicant to crew now runs end to end inside our own tools, and two manual steps (checking Employment Hero by hand, and chasing signed contracts over email) are gone.*

## What shipped

- **Employment Hero self-setup is tracked automatically.** When a new starter finishes their EH self-setup — personal details, bank, tax, super — THE GOAT flags it with a green **EH ✓** badge on the Recruitment tab. No more logging into KeyPay to check; it's detected in the background and updates on its own.

- **Crew contracts are now signed in the app — no more emailed Google Docs.** Marking a candidate **Sent to EH** now asks for their **commencement date**, then emails them a link to read and electronically sign their employment contract. Once signed, a **Contract ✓** badge appears, and you can open the signed PDF straight from their detail panel via **View signed contract**. The signed copy is stored securely with a full audit trail — who signed, when, and from where.

- **New "All Docs Received" stage.** A candidate moves into this pile automatically once **both** their EH self-setup is complete **and** their contract is signed — whichever lands last trips it. It's your at-a-glance "ready to convert" list.

- **Convert is gated on All Docs Received.** You can now only **Convert to crew** once a candidate is fully documented — EH *and* contract in. If Convert isn't available yet, the **EH ✓** / **Contract ✓** badges show exactly what's still outstanding, so no one gets converted half-documented.

## Under the hood
The backend, Supabase edge functions and the completion poller that drive all of the above are already live in production. This build brings the ops-facing side into THE GOAT: the badges, the new pile, the commencement-date input, the signed-contract link, and the convert gate. Electronic signatures are captured in line with the Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth).
