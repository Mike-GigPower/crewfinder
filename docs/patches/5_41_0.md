# 5.41.0

*Released 10 September 2026*

A new lane on the Operations tab: Shadowlands — active crew with no Crew Hub
push subscription who have something riding on it in the next four weeks.

With SMS retired, push is the only channel we have, so "no push on file" now
means "cannot be told anything". The lane splits that into two problems, because
they need different things done about them:

- Unanswered offer — an open offer sitting with someone who was never told we
  asked. It is already dead, and it will keep counting on Confirmations to chase
  until somebody rings them.
- Booked only — confirmed on a call, but a time change, a venue change or a
  cancellation has no way of reaching them.
- Offer + booked — both.

The badge counts people to ring, not a percentage. Above the donuts, a coverage
strip carries the roster picture: how many active crew have push, how many do
not, how many of those have work coming, and how many open offers are sitting
with crew who cannot be told about them.

Clicking the lane, or any segment of either donut, opens the list with EIN, name
and phone number, soonest commitment first. The number copies with a click.

At release: 274 of 426 active crew have push (64%). 152 do not — 22 of them have
work in the next four weeks, and 26 of the 74 open offers are with crew who were
never told we asked.
