# 4.0.0

*Released 14 July 2026*

v4.0.0 — Licence management + Administration records
## Licences now flow through onboarding

Converting a candidate to crew now brings their licences across to SmartStaff
automatically — each licence's certificate PDF and certified date included, and
attached to the crew member's Licenses tab. The convert screen reports what was
added so you can see it landed.

## Manage crew licences

Crew records now have a Licences view — add, edit and remove a crew member's
licences, including their certified and expiry dates.

## Manage Customers, Venues & Contacts

The Administration → Records cards are now full management screens, not just
"add". Search, view and edit any customer, venue or contact — and remove them two
ways:

- **Set inactive** — the everyday way to retire a record. Reversible, keeps all
  history, drops it from the default list.
- **Delete** — for records created in error. Guarded: anything still tied to
  bookings or history (a venue with inductions, a customer with jobs, a contact on
  a booking) can't be removed by accident — you'll be told what's still linked and
  pointed at "set inactive" instead.
