# 4.5.2

*Released 17 July 2026*

THE GOAT v4.5.2 — Day-first commencement dates

A small usability follow-up to v4.5.1. GOAT-only — backend and onboarding are unchanged.

What changed


Commencement dates are now entered day-first (DD-MM-YYYY). When you mark an applicant (or a batch) Sent to EH, the start-date prompt now takes the Australian format — e.g. 01-09-2026 for 1 September 2026 — instead of YYYY-MM-DD. Impossible dates (like 31-02-2026) and wrong formats are rejected, and editing an existing date shows it day-first too. Behind the scenes the date is still stored and merged into the contract unchanged, so nothing else is affected.
