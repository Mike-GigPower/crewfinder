# 5.112.0

*Released 19 November 2026*

Crew on standby can now take themselves off it, and THE GOAT shows when they have.

- **Left standby:** a new status for a backup who has taken themselves off standby from Crew Hub. It shows greyed in the call dialog and Responses tab, with the time in the tooltip, and has its own chip next to Declined. It doesn't count as a decline, and it doesn't count against their reliability.
- **Linked calls:** leaving standby on a load out also takes them off standby on the load-in that feeds it, because they couldn't be promoted there without the load out.
- **Re-offering:** change a Left standby row to Unconfirmed, and THE GOAT offers to send a new offer, the same as for a Declined row.
- **Pushes:** someone who has left standby isn't re-notified when a call's offers are re-sent to everyone.
- **Crew lists and stand-downs** leave them out, and Crew Finder treats them like a decline.
- The Crew Hub button arrives with the next Crew Hub update. Until then, nobody can leave standby on their own.
