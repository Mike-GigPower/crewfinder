# 4.34.0

*Released 10 August 2026*

Ops tab: two induction lanes

Bookings at risk — crew booked on upcoming calls at induction venues who have no induction on file, or whose induction lapses before the call runs. The second case is new: an induction that's valid today but expires before the job is a risk nothing previously caught.

Inductions expiring — crew whose induction expires in the next fortnight, one row per person listing every venue affected.

Inductions now resolve across venue groups, so a Melbourne Park induction correctly covers all five arenas.
