# 4.24.0

*Released 6 August 2026*

Fatigue and travel warnings on the Schedule can now be acknowledged. Click a warning against a crew member, confirm, and optionally record why the arrangement is fine — the warning goes muted grey and shows who signed it off and when. Clicking again offers to undo it.

Acknowledgements are specific: they cover one crew member on one pair of calls under one rule. If either call is later retimed, the acknowledgement lapses and the warning returns at full weight, because the arrangement that was signed off no longer exists.

Genuine double-bookings (Rule 1) cannot be acknowledged — they have to be resolved. The rolling 24-hour limit (Rule 4) can't be acknowledged either, for now.
