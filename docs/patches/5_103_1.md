# 5.103.1

*Released 12 November 2026*

The booking's Financials tab now shows quoted hours (as billed) beside scheduled and actual hours for each call type, and the empty button under an imported quote's amount has gone.

**Hours by call type** has a new **Quoted h (billed)** column, between Calls and Scheduled h. It is what the Estimator quote priced for that call type: the quoted crew count multiplied by the hours each one was billed for, added up across the quote's lines. The column total always equals the quote, so a call type the quote priced but the booking never called gets its own row, with no calls and no scheduled or actual hours against it.

Quoted hours include the minimum billable hours when the quote was priced with them, so a figure higher than Scheduled h is normal and does not mean the schedule disagrees with the quote. A four-hour minimum on a two-hour call is quoted at four and scheduled at two.

The column reads "—" where there is nothing behind it to show: a booking with no quote, an amount entered by hand, or a quote imported before 5.102.0, when the Estimator did not yet send its per-line hours. The paid-cancellation row reads "—" as well, since nothing in the quote priced it.

Under an imported quote's amount there used to be a small blank button. Clicking it offered a box to type an amount into, and saving was then refused — an imported figure is frozen in the Estimator and cannot be overwritten by hand. That button has gone. A booking with no quote still offers **Set amount…**, and a hand-entered amount still offers **Change…**.
