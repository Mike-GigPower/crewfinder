# 5.94.0

*Released 7 November 2026*

Crew Boss No-show reports now show in Times outstanding, and Ops confirm or reject them in Enter Times.

When a crew boss reports someone as a No-show, the call's card in Times outstanding shows "N No-show to confirm". In Enter Times that person's row is marked **NO-SHOW REPORTED**, with who reported it, when, and the boss's note, and their times are locked until the report is resolved.

**Confirm No-show** marks them as a No-show on the call.

**Not a No-show** rejects the report. If the boss entered times for them before reporting them, those times come back, marked "Restored from the boss's earlier entry". Otherwise they are owed times again.

**Accept Times** is held until every report on the call has been confirmed or rejected. Confirming and rejecting are admin-only.
