# 5.103.0

*Released 12 November 2026*

New crew now get access to the Employment Hero employee portal automatically when they are converted to crew.

Until now, Employment Hero created a new starter's employee record and sent them their self-setup email, but never gave them access to the employee portal, where they see their payslips and keep their details up to date. That was a manual step in Employment Hero that was easy to miss.

Converting a candidate to crew now grants that access as the last step, after the welcome email. Employment Hero sends the crew member its own email with a link to activate their portal login. It works the same way for a returning crew member whose existing record is updated, and if they already have portal access nothing is sent twice.

The convert summary has a new **EH portal access** line saying whether access was granted, was already in place, or did not go through and why. On a converted crew member's row, Admins see **EH portal ✓** with the date once access is in place, or a **Grant EH portal access** button to try again.

Access is only ever granted to the email address Employment Hero already holds for that employee, and only when it matches the email on their application. If the two differ, nothing is granted and the line says to check the record in Employment Hero.

Everyone converted before this release has already been given access.
