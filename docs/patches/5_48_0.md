# 5.48.0

*Released 16 September 2026*

The Recruitment tab's search box now finds a candidate by their EIN.

An EIN is only assigned at Convert to crew, so this finds people who have
already been through the pipeline and now sit in the Crew pile. Their EIN is
shown on the row itself as well, in grey beside the name, so a search result is
legible at a glance without opening the record.

The EIN has to be typed in full. A partial number is ignored, because a two or
three digit fragment matches too many employee numbers to be useful. Name,
reference, email and phone all still match on part of what you type, exactly as
before.

Searching from a pile other than Crew behaves as it always has: the "matches in
other piles" line appears above the list, and one click widens the search.
