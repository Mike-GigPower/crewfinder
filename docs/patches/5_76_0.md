# 5.76.0

*Released 20 October 2026*

THE GOAT now reads rates, public holidays and the Christmas and New Year double-penalty times live from the Estimator, so a rate change no longer needs an update.

- **Rate card on each booking:** a booking can be marked as an M&OP booking, with a required Australian Open Yes / No. Imports from the Estimator fill this in automatically. Only Admins can change it afterwards, and every change is logged.
- **GM Performance forward view:** each call is priced on its booking's rate card, with the Estimator's public holidays and the Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve double rate.
- **Administration › Reference data:** shows where the rates came from and when they last changed, and can check THE GOAT's sums against the Estimator's.
- Crew marked with the new Cancelled status will show as "Cancelled" once late-cancellation pay goes live.
