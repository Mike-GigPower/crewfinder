# 4.36.0

*Released 10 August 2026*

Enter Times now only writes times for crew who are Confirmed on the call. Declined, no-showed and unresponded crew render read-only, are skipped by the fill-down arrow, and are left untouched when you save.

The timesheet import takes the other approach, because the Crew Boss's sheet is evidence of who actually worked: anyone on the sheet who isn't Confirmed in SmartStaff is flagged with their status and ticked to write, so you can untick them or fix their status first.

Also fixes a long-standing defect where saving times could stamp a paygrade and rate columns onto crew who had declined the shift.
