# 5.58.0

*Released 22 September 2026*

Fully documented applicants can now be moved back to Ready to convert. Applicants whose start date is several weeks away are often parked on Hold, and until now there was no way to move them forward again. A Ready to convert button now appears on those applicants, once their Employment Hero setup and signed contract are both on file.

Also fixed: moving an applicant back through Attended no longer re-sends the details form to someone who has already submitted it.
