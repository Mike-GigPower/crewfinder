# 5.3.0

*Released 14 August 2026*

Crew detail panel. Click any crew name in Crew Finder to open a full-width panel beneath the row, in either view. Shows a Gantt of every shift within 48 hours either side of the call you're filling, with unavailability blocks, the target call marked, and a photo and reliability strip. The hover card also gains a compact ±48h shift list.

Schedule call counts corrected. The per-call badges now count from the actual roster rather than a scraped banner — the amber unconfirmed count was silently reading zero, so calls could show four open slots when twenty-one were open. A no-show count appears when viewing past days; the redundant unfilled badge is gone.

Push reachability. Crew with no Crew Hub push subscription now carry a quiet marker in Crew Finder, with the detail spelled out in the crew panel. Requires no action from crew — it reflects what the Hub already knows.
