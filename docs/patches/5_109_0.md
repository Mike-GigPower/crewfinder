# 5.109.0

*Released 17 November 2026*

Admins can now change a crew member's pay grade from their crew record, and THE GOAT moves the calls they are already booked on to match.

- **Where:** Crew → Records → a crew member → Details → Pay grade → Change pay grade. Admins only.
- **The ladder:** T1/T2 → T1B/T2B when probation finishes, T1B/T2B → T1A/T2A for the leadership team. Sun/Pub Hol rows step the same way (Sun/Pub Hol → HolB → HolA). Role grades such as Fork, Steel Hand and Crew Boss are never touched.
- **With effect from:** defaults to today and can go back to the start of the current pay week (Tuesday), never earlier and never in the future.
- **What changes:** rows on unlocked calls from that date, for Unconfirmed, Pending, Confirmed, Backup and Cancelled crew. Each row's grade and its four rates move together. Calls booked above the new grade are listed unticked; untick anything else you want left as it is.
- **What doesn't:** locked calls are listed to fix by hand in SmartStaff before the pay run, and calls before the WEF date are shown for reference only.
- **Safety net:** if any calls are still on an older grade than the person's own (for example after a grade was changed directly in SmartStaff), the crew record shows a banner with a Review link. Calls you chose to keep are not counted again.
- Every change is logged with who made it and what happened to each call.
- Employment Hero is not updated yet; change the pay rate template and Level tag there as before.
