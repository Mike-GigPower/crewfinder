# 5.60.0

*Released 23 September 2026*

You can now message a crew member straight from their record in THE GOAT. Open their record and click Message: it goes to their Crew Hub messages and, if they have notifications on, to their phone. The panel shows whether they have notifications on and lists what has been sent to them before, with a tick once they have read it.

When you delete a licence, THE GOAT offers to tell the person, with a draft you can edit before sending. For a Working with Children Check it explains that Gig Power needs an Employee card, not a Volunteer one. Nothing is sent without you pressing Send.

On Crew, Records, clicking a licence chip such as WWCC now opens that person's record on the Licences tab with that licence highlighted.
