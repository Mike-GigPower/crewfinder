# 5.77.1

*Released 20 October 2026*

Import Times now skips cancelled calls, so a timesheet can never overwrite the times on a call that was stood down.

- **Cancelled calls are skipped on import:** a sheet tab that belongs to a cancelled call is no longer written, whether it is matched by Call ID or by crew. The review says "N cancelled call(s) skipped — check their times in Enter Times" and names the calls. Cancelled calls are also left out of the list for choosing a call by hand.
- **Charge-only cancellations in Enter Times:** when the customer is charged but crew are not paid, stood-down crew show as CANCELLED · CHARGED rather than CANCELLED · PAID, with a note that crew pay must be zeroed in SmartStaff. (This shipped in 5.77.0 but was missing from its notes.)
