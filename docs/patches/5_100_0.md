# 5.100.0

*Released 10 November 2026*

Crew records now have a **Dietary** tab, next to Emergency, so the office can see and record what crew can and can't eat.

**The Dietary tab.** It shows what the crew member has told us: the options they ticked, their note, and when it was last updated and by whom (Crew Hub, the apply form, or Gig Power office). **Edit** lets admin and operations tick Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Halal, Kosher, Food allergy or intolerance, or Other. Food allergy and Other need a note saying what it is. **No requirements** records that they've told us they have none, which is different from "Not answered yet".

**Entered by the office.** Anything the office enters is marked "awaiting their confirmation in Crew Hub" until the crew member confirms it there. Until then it isn't printed on client crew lists.

**The fork-and-knife icon.** It shows beside the EIN and ID on the crew record and beside the name in a call's Booked Crew list when someone has dietary requirements. Hover to see what they are. Click it on the crew record to open the Dietary tab.

**Crew lists.** Create Crew Lists has a new **Dietary** column, ticked by default. It prints each person's requirements and note, with a count under each call, such as "Dietary: 2 Vegetarian · 1 Gluten-free". Requirements the crew member hasn't confirmed yet are left out, and the list says how many.

**Convert.** When an applicant has answered the dietary question on the apply form, convert copies their answer onto the new crew record. This starts working once the apply form asks the question.

Dietary information is for admin and operations only. It never appears on Crew Finder and is never sent to Ask the GOAT.
