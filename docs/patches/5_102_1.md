# 5.102.1

*Released 12 November 2026*

The call dialog is wider, so each Booked Crew row fits on one line.

Phone numbers no longer break across two lines beside long names, and the call dialog now opens a little wider to make room for them. The ✕ that removes someone from a call is no longer hidden under the scrollbar in Safari.

The same scrollbar fix applies to the tabs in the Booking and Crew record dialogs, so nothing at the right edge of a tab sits underneath the scrollbar.
