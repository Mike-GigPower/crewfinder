# 5.89.0

*Released 27 October 2026*

**The Responses tab now shows answers to time changes.** When a call's time moves after someone has accepted, their answer appears under their accept time: declined time change (in red), accepted new time, cleared by ops, or time changed — no answer yet (in amber). Hover the line to see every change and what the time was before. Answers are recorded from 27 October 2026, so an earlier time-change decline still shows only as Declined beside the original accept time.
