# 4.31.0

*Released 9 August 2026*

New: Ops tab

A new leftmost tab showing what needs action, starting with open offers awaiting a reply. Shows a count, a status and age breakdown, and every outstanding offer as a card. Click a chart segment to filter the list; click a card to open that call.

This is the first slice of an operations landing page — more action areas to follow.
