# 5.31.1

*Released 5 September 2026*

Fixes a SmartStaff bug where a crew member who confirmed a shift and later declined it stayed unbookable across that window. The calendar row is kept deliberately so ops can still see the decline, but it was also refusing every overlapping add — reported as a clash with a confirmed shift that no longer existed. 52 crew were affected across 106 future shifts at the time of release.

Confirmed shifts and unavailability periods still block, unchanged. Being a backup does not block.

The add-failure message no longer claims a cause SmartStaff never reports.
