# 5.4.0

*Released 17 August 2026*

Replaces the six-value licence list with a 41-code catalogue covering the WorkSafe high-risk work classes, Construction Induction, Working at Heights, EWP, First Aid, Traffic Control, Working with Children, and the five driver classes.

Adds a Licence Triage screen under Administration for confirming recorded licences against the card image, including splitting a single record into multiple licences where the card carries more than one.

Existing licence records have been mapped to canonical codes where the recorded text was unambiguous; the rest are queued for triage. Manage Crew licence dropdowns now build from the catalogue rather than a hardcoded list.
