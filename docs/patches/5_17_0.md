# 5.17.0

*Released 25 August 2026*

The Inductions expiring lane now shows a second donut: which venues the expiring inductions are at. Clicking a venue filters the list below, and it combines with the lead-time filter. The two donuts show different totals on purpose — lead time counts people, venue spread counts person-and-venue pairs, so someone expiring at two venues appears once in the list and twice in the venue chart.
