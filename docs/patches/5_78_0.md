# 5.78.0

*Released 21 October 2026*

Every call now has a Call Type: the rate card role it charges at, kept separate from its name. Nothing is priced from it yet: SmartStaff still bills and pays from its RATE column until THE GOAT takes over pricing.

- **Call Type on calls:** set from the estimate on import, and required when you add a call or create a booking by hand. Older calls show a type worked out from their name ("from call name"), or "not set".
- **Admins can change a call's type** in the call dialog. A call whose type isn't on the booking's rate card is marked "off card".
- **Rate override (Admin):** charge or pay one crew member on a call differently.
- **Team Lead and Crew Boss:** the boss tick is now called Team Lead (no change to what it does). Crew Boss is new: it charges and pays that crew member as Crew Boss. Not offered on M&OP bookings.
- **Clearer buttons:** Team Lead, Crew Boss and Rate look different when they're on.
- **Call names are free text** when adding a call.
