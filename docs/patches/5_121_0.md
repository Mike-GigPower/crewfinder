# 5.121.0

*Released 24 November 2026*

Crew lists now come as a spreadsheet as well as a printable page. Create Crew Lists has two buttons: **📄 PDF / Print** opens the list in a new tab exactly as before, and **📊 CSV** downloads the same list — same calls, same columns, same order — as a file you can edit in Excel or Numbers. The write-in columns come through empty, ready to fill in, and phone numbers and EINs keep their leading zero. As after a printed list, Admins are asked whether it was sent to the client.

Every call's header block now names the **on-site contact** and their phone, on the printed list and in the CSV. When a booking has no on-site contact the list prints Gig Power and the office number, 03 9376 9500, so nobody at a venue is left without someone to ring. An on-site contact with no number on file keeps their own name, rather than being shown beside the office number.

Above the calls to include there are now **Select all** and **Clear** links. Select all ticks every call except cancelled ones.

Booking documents can be filed under a new **Schedule** category, listed after Requirements.
