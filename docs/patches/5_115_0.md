# 5.115.0

*Released 20 November 2026*

Import Times can now read times straight from the Crew Hub check-in sheet. On a booking using live check-in, open the booking, choose Import Times, then Import from check-in. Each call's times, breaks (split into day and night by when they were taken) and late marks come across for review, and anything that needs a look is flagged in amber and left unticked before anything is written.

Live check-in: a booking can now be switched to Live, where the check-in sheet is the timesheet and no Google Sheet is used. The booking dialog shows Off, Shadow or Live, and making a Google Sheet for a Live booking asks first.
