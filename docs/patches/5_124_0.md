# 5.124.0

*Released 25 November 2026*

A crew member the Crew Boss marked Left early on the check-in sheet is now paid the hours they worked when you import times from check-in. The 4-hour minimum is not added for them.

Crew Hub's check-in sheet has a new Left early mark on each person's row. The Crew Boss picks why they left: injured, sick, sent home, personal or other. An injury always carries its Mitta incident reference. Being sent home by Gig Power and leaving by choice are both paid as worked.

In Import from check-in, that person's row keeps the times they actually worked, even on a call booked for 4 hours or more where everyone else is topped up to the call's start plus 4 hours. The row stays ticked, with a note giving the reason, for example "left early (Injured, Mitta M-1234; rolled ankle) — paid as worked, no 4-hour minimum". Untick it if you want to look into it first.

Everyone else on the call is unchanged: a call booked for 4 hours or more is still topped to its start plus 4 hours, a late starter still gets that same finish, and a call booked for under 4 hours is still paid as worked.
