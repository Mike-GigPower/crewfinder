# 5.53.0

*Released 19 September 2026*

A KeyPay name mismatch can now be confirmed or corrected instead of stopping the setup, and bookings understand Seamstress calls.

## KeyPay setup no longer dead-ends on a name

When the Employment Hero record linked to a candidate carries a different name, the setup screen used to offer nothing but Close. It now shows the two records side by side and, where the email addresses match, offers a way forward: continue with the discrepancy confirmed, or adopt the Employment Hero spelling onto the candidate record.

This is the common case rather than an edge case. An application form that includes a middle name will differ from the legal name the employee types during their own Employment Hero setup.

Where the email addresses differ, the screen stays terminal. A mismatched email means the stored employee ID may point at someone else entirely, and that is checked in KeyPay rather than waved through.

A confirmed override stays visible on screen right through to the payroll write, and is recorded against the person who approved it.

## Seamstress calls

Estimates can now carry a Seamstress call, and it comes through the booking import like any other. Seamstress is also available when building a booking by hand. Wardrobe remains a valid call name and now prices as Show Crew, matching the Estimator.
