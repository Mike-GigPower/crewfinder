# 5.62.0

*Released 24 September 2026*

Crew notes now sit in the crew record header, between Reliability and the photo, so they stay in view on every tab. The photo is now the same height as the Notes box.

Utilization, Inductions and Records now show the same first columns: Name, Phone, star rating and EIN. Hover a crew member's name on any of the three pages to see the same card as in Crew Finder: their photo, late and no-show counts, shifts in the 48 hours either side of now, and notes.

Records also shows each person's expired and expiring licences, their late and no-show counts for the last 12 months, and whether they have Crew Hub notifications on. Click a column heading to sort by it.

On all three pages, the search box comes first and finds crew by name, EIN or phone number. The group chips sit in a row underneath with a minimum star filter on the right, and the Name, star and EIN headings sort when clicked. The search, group and star filters carry across as you switch between the three pages, and so does the sort order.

Each page now says how many crew are shown and, when a filter is on, which one, with a Clear filters button. The group, star filter and sort are remembered next time you open THE GOAT. Records has an Export CSV button that saves exactly what is on screen, and the Active and Inactive buttons now sit beside the search. You can also Tab to a crew member's name to see their card, and press Enter to open their record.

In the Message panel, the Title and Message fields now dim once a message has been sent.
