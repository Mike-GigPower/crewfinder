# 3.21.0

*Released 10 July 2026*

## THE GOAT v3.21.0

Crew Finder now surfaces each crew member's reliability history at a glance, and the
Recruitment tab gains a batch of workflow improvements — bookings import, an Attended
stage, batch actions, stage-aging flags, and a Withdrawn pile.

### Crew Finder
- **Late / No-show on the hover card.** Hovering a crew name now shows their Late and
  No-show tallies on the card. Anyone with more than a year of history gets both a
  last-12-months and an all-time line; newer crew get a single all-time line. The
  numbers match their SmartStaff profile.

### Recruitment
- **Import bookings from TryBooking.** Drop a TryBooking Attendee List CSV to mark
  matching candidates Booked in one pass. Matches on Gig Power reference first, falls
  back to a unique booking email, routes anything with a shared email to a review list
  (never auto-booked), skips anyone already past Invited, and previews every change
  before you confirm.
- **Session date captured on import.** The import reads the session time from the
  report, shows it in the preview to sanity-check, and stamps it onto each booked
  candidate — recorded for later without ever blocking a booking if the date can't be read.
- **Attended stage + batch actions.** A new Attended pile with a Mark attended button,
  plus row checkboxes, select-all, and a batch bar to Invite / Hold / Not suitable many
  candidates at once — built for 30–50-person intakes. Selection is per-pile, so a batch
  action can't land on the wrong list.
- **Time-in-stage + aging flag.** Each candidate shows how long they've sat in their
  current stage, turning amber when they've waited too long (Invited ≥ 7 days, Booked
  ≥ 14, Attended ≥ 7) so no one goes cold.
- **Withdrawn pile.** Candidates who opt out via the apply site now appear in a
  read-only Withdrawn pile showing their reason and date — no triage buttons, just a
  single Back to New in case of a mistake.
