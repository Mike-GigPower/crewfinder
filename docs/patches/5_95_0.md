# 5.95.0

*Released 9 November 2026*

New on a booking's **Crew bosses** tab: **Link to Crew Boss calls** (Admin). It lists every call that overlaps exactly one Crew Boss call on the booking, so you can link them in one go. Untick any call that really has no Crew Boss before you press Link, and choose whether to leave the unticked calls as they are or mark them **No Crew Boss**.

A call marked **No Crew Boss** shows a grey chip on the Calls list, drops out of the amber "Not linked" warning, and isn't offered by the Link button again. **Undo** on the chip removes the mark, and linking the call to its Crew Boss call removes it too.

Unlinking a call that the Link button would offer marks it No Crew Boss, so it isn't offered again. THE GOAT asks before it does.

The amber "Not linked" banner now lets you tick calls and mark them No Crew Boss together, and has a link to the Crew bosses tab.

Calls that overlap two Crew Boss calls, and calls that have already started, aren't offered: choose their Crew Boss call in the editor as before. A link to a Crew Boss call with nobody confirmed yet takes effect once someone is confirmed. Nothing is linked unless someone presses Link.
