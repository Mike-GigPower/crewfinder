# 4.23.0

*Released 6 August 2026*

The Schedule now tells a genuine double-booking apart from a policy warning. A crew member whose times actually overlap shows a red ⚡ clash; a long-shift rest gap, a tight venue change, or over 16 hours in a rolling 24 shows an amber ⚠ warning. A spot operator rolling into their own load-out is no longer flagged the same way as someone booked in two places at once.

Hovering a flagged crew member now shows the specific rule that fired and why — previously that reason was computed and then discarded before it reached the screen. Where one person trips more than one rule, all reasons appear in the same tooltip.

The legend gains a separate Warning swatch alongside Clash.
