# 5.43.0

*Released 12 September 2026*

Crew Finder's "Hide from results" filter now works when more than one call is selected.

Previously, with two or more calls selected, "Declined this call" did nothing at all. The filter read the calendar, and a declined assignment has no calendar entry — so declines were invisible and people who had already said no kept appearing in the results. "Already booked" kept working, which is why the pair looked half-broken rather than broken.

The filter also required someone to have answered the same way on every selected call. Anyone booked on the load-in and declined on the load-out was shown, despite having nothing left to be offered.

Both are fixed. The filter now reads the real booking statuses rather than inferring them from the calendar, and hides anyone who has answered every selected call — booked or declined, and not necessarily the same way on each. Crew still free for any selected call stay in the list, exactly as before.

Reported by Joe, 11 September 2026. App-only release — no SmartStaff or database changes.
