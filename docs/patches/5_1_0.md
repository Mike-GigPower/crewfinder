# 5.1.0

*Released 12 August 2026*

Manage Crew is now a view, not a buried dialog. It lives under Crew as Records, alongside Utilization and Inductions, instead of behind a card in Administration. Same list, same edit screen — just where you'd expect to find it.

Filters carry across all three Crew views. Search a name or pick a group on any of Records, Utilization or Inductions, and the others already have it applied. Records still matches on EIN and phone as well as name.

Inactive crew. Switching Records to Inactive now greys out Utilization and Inductions, since inactive crew aren't scheduled or inducted — rather than showing you two empty tables.
