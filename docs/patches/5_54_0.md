# 5.54.0

*Released 20 September 2026*

Open offers now show in Crew Finder.

Until now the Finder worked out who was free from confirmed bookings alone. An offer that had gone out but not yet been answered wrote nothing to the calendar, so somebody sitting on three unanswered offers across a Saturday read as completely free.

Anyone holding an open offer that would clash with the call you are filling is now marked with an envelope beside their name. Hovering it says which call, when and where, and what the problem would be - an overlap, too short a turnaround, a venue change, or too many hours inside twenty-four.

They stay bookable. An offer is not a booking and may well be declined, so the Finder tells you and leaves the call with you.

Offered shifts also appear on the timeline as dotted outlines, and in the crew card tagged OFFER alongside the confirmed ones, so the shape of somebody's day is visible before you add to it.

This closes the gap that let one crew member accumulate twenty hours inside twenty-four across separate calls, which only became clear afterwards.
