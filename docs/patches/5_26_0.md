# 5.26.0

*Released 2 September 2026*

Crew are now told automatically when a call is stood down or reinstated.

Standing a call down sends each person a notification on Crew Hub, and the wording depends on what they were actually holding: someone who had accepted the shift is told it is **cancelled**, while someone who only had an outstanding offer is told the **offer was withdrawn** — they never had a shift to lose.

Reinstating a call tells everyone it is **back on and to accept again**, which is what actually needs to happen, because nobody comes back confirmed.

**The result panel now reports what really happened.** It names anyone who could not be reached — usually someone who has not turned notifications on in Crew Hub — with their number and the reason, and shows everyone else quietly as notified. Until now it told you to ring every single person on every cancellation, whether or not that was true.

Notifications carry the call name and start time, so "Load In · Thu 3 Sep 08:00" says which shift rather than leaving it ambiguous for anyone working several Load Ins that week.

Also in this release: both cancellation endpoints return the call's start time, and the notification no longer repeats the "CANCELLED - " prefix that SmartStaff puts on the call name.
