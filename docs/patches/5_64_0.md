# 5.64.0

*Released 1 October 2026*

Recruitment has a new Waiting on us pile, first in the row of piles. It lists every candidate whose next step is ours, across all stages: Ready to convert (phone and convert), Details in with a venue induction uploaded (send to EH), Booked into a session that has already run (mark attendance), and New, while an upcoming session has seats (invite). Each row shows what we owe and how long it has waited, turning amber and then red as it ages, with the longest waits first.

On Today, Operations, a new Recruitment — waiting on us card shows the same total with two charts: which action is needed, and how many are on time, ageing or overdue. Click the card to open the pile, or click a chart segment to open it narrowed to just those candidates. The card shows for operations and admin logins only.
