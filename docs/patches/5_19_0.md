# 5.19.0

*Released 27 August 2026*

Unavailability now lists chronologically, with the year shown on every date — previously only day and month appeared, so periods from different years looked out of order even though they weren't. Current and upcoming periods are separated, and past ones are collapsed behind a toggle and grouped by year.

New calendar view on the Edit Crew Member Unavailability tab. Month grid, opens on whatever is current or next rather than today. Full days fill solid, partial days show their hours. Click a day to see the reason and remove it.

Crew Utilization now goes out to 90 days instead of 28.
