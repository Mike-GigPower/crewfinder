# 5.33.0

*Released 7 September 2026*

Applicants who couldn't check in at the induction — the QR wouldn't scan, they
lost their reference, or they walked in without a booking — can now be marked
attended from the Recruitment tab whatever pile they are sitting in.

"Mark attended" previously appeared only on Invited and Booked applicants.
Anyone in New or Hold had no route to Attended at all: no button, nothing to
click. It is now offered from New, Invited, Booked and Hold. Not suitable still
needs Return to New first — deliberately two steps.

Because marking someone attended emails them their details form, and the button
now sits on piles you triage quickly, it asks you to confirm first and names the
address it is about to email.

Note: the details email sends once per applicant. If someone has already had
one, use "Resend details form" on their row rather than re-marking them.
