# 5.97.0

*Released 10 November 2026*

Crew → Records has a new Last shift column, between Reliability 12 mo and Push, showing the date of each crew member's most recent worked shift.

A worked shift is a confirmed shift dated today or earlier, the same rule as the Hours tab. Cancelled calls, no-shows and future shifts don't count. Hover the date to see the booking, call and start time; click it to open that call. Dates more than 12 months ago show in amber, and "Never" means no confirmed shift on record.

The new "No shift in 12 months" filter shows crew whose last shift was over 12 months ago, including those who have never had one. The column sorts, and Export CSV gains Last shift date and Last shift columns.
