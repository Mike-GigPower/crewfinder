# 5.55.0

*Released 21 September 2026*

The Crew Finder timeline view now gives the schedule the whole window.

**The schedule strip stretches to fill whatever space is left** instead of sitting at a fixed width. On a typical laptop that is roughly 40 hours of schedule on screen where it used to be 24, and it re-measures when you resize the window.

**A new Columns menu**, beside Table and Timeline, hides Phone, Rating, Groups and Detail. Every column you hide goes straight to the schedule, and a badge on the button shows how many are hidden so nothing is quietly missing.

**The crew name column can be resized** by dragging the handle on its right edge, or double-clicked to fit the longest name on screen. Arrow keys nudge it when the handle has focus.

**The checkbox and name columns stay put** when the schedule scrolls sideways, so you never lose track of whose row you are reading.

**Collapse the Unfilled Calls sidebar** with the Calls button in the toolbar to hand another 320px to the schedule.

**Induction badges are shorter in this view.** They keep their tick or warning icon, their colour and their status word, but the venue name moves into the tooltip — that text was squeezing crew names down to a single letter. Table view still shows the full label, and hovering any badge gives you the whole thing.

**The crew photo card opens beside the name column** rather than on top of the badges, and hovering a badge now shows its own tooltip instead of opening the card.

Your hidden columns, name column width and sidebar state are remembered per person.
