# 5.102.0

*Released 11 November 2026*

Bookings gain two new tabs, Documents and Financials. They sit at the end of the row: Details, Calls, Crew Notes, Call feeds, Crew bosses, Documents, Financials.

**Documents** keeps everything that belongs to a booking in one place — quotes, invoices, requirements, special conditions and site information — as either uploaded files or links. Everyone who can open a booking can see and open its documents. Admins add and remove them.

Files can be PDF, JPEG, PNG, DOCX or XLSX, up to 20 MB. A file opens through a private link that lasts five minutes, so a copied URL stops working shortly afterwards. Removing a document hides it from the list but keeps the file, so anything taken off by mistake can be restored.

A booking imported from an Estimator quote shows that quote as a fixed first row, so nobody has to add it by hand.

**Financials** is for Admins only and shows a booking's money, all ex GST:

- **Quoted** — the total frozen into the Estimator quote when the booking was imported, or an amount an Admin types in for older and hand-made bookings. The figure always says where it came from.
- **Expected invoice** — what the booking should invoice to, worked out from the crew's keyed times and the current rate card.
- **Invoiced** — what SmartStaff actually invoiced.
- **Cost** — crew pay, as SmartStaff calculated it.
- **Margin**, and how quoted compares with invoiced.

Underneath is a call-by-call table that flags any call where the expected and actual figures disagree by more than five cents, and a table of hours by call type with scheduled and actual side by side.

Financials is deliberately cautious about what it does not know. A booking part-way through never looks finished: anything missing shows as a dash with the reason, never as zero. Calls dated before the current rate card came into effect on 1 July 2026 show no expected figure at all, because pricing them on today's rates would invent a difference that was never real. Paid cancellations get their own row in the hours table, and crew who were promoted off standby but have not replied are left out of scheduled hours, the same way the call dialog counts them.
