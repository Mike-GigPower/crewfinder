# 4.32.0

*Released 9 August 2026*

Ops tab: Calls to fill

A second lane showing calls short of crew — how many positions are open, how soon the call is, and which shortfalls already have someone offered against them. Calls with nobody asked yet are called out, since those are the ones needing Crew Finder.

Also: both lanes now load in sequence, and filtering one no longer affects the other.
