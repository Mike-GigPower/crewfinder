# 4.5.1

*Released 17 July 2026*

# THE GOAT v4.5.1 — Recruitment action-bar fixes

*A small follow-up to v4.5.0 — the Recruitment action bar now shows the right actions for each stage, and a stray button is gone. Backend and onboarding are unchanged; this is a GOAT-only update.*

## What changed

- **The batch action bar is now stage-aware.** When you tick applicants, the actions offered now match their stage instead of always showing the New-applicant set. **Details Submitted** or **Attended** applicants now offer **Mark Sent to EH**; **New** offers Invite to induction; **Invited** offers Re-invite; a mixed-stage selection shows only the actions valid for everyone (Hold / Not suitable). No more Invite/Hold/Not suitable showing on candidates who are well past that point.

- **Send a whole intake to EH in one go.** Batch **Mark Sent to EH** asks once for a single **commencement date** and applies it to everyone selected — built for the monthly casual intake, where the whole cohort commences on the same date (they're only paid once they actually work a shift). Each person is sent to EH and emailed their contract to sign. The per-applicant "Mark Sent to EH" on the detail panel still works for one-off sends between intakes.

- **Fixed a stray "undefined" button.** An extra button labelled *undefined* was appearing on the applicant detail panel (a side effect of the new All Docs Received stage). It's gone — the detail panel now shows only the valid triage actions.
