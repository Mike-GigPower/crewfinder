# 5.7.2

*Released 20 August 2026*

Fixes three places where content ran off the bottom of the window with no way to reach it.

The Crew Finder Filters panel now scrolls when it's taller than the window. With a call selected the licence filters appear, which pushed the distance slider and the Venue/Postcode origin buttons out of view entirely.

The Recruitment applicant list now sizes itself to the space actually available rather than a fixed 70% of window height. Piles that fell just under that threshold — New in particular, especially with the batch action bar showing — were clipped with no scrollbar, while longer piles like Invited and Booked scrolled normally.

The admin access modal now caps its height and scrolls, matching the other admin dialogs.
