# 4.16.0

*Released 30 July 2026*

Induction expiry now uses each venue's real validity period, read from the
induction catalogue in SmartStaff rather than values hardcoded in the app.

This corrects Palais Theatre (24 months) and Federation Square (36 months),
which were both being calculated at 12 months. 19 crew were showing as expired
when their inductions were still valid.

The Induction Checker, the crew self-view and Crew Finder availability now share
a single expiry calculation, matching the crew-facing pages exactly.
