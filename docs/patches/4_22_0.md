# 4.22.0

*Released 6 August 2026*

A Dashboard view for Recruitment. The tab now has two views: Operations, which is everything you already use, and Dashboard, which shows how the funnel is actually performing. Your choice is remembered, so it opens where you left it. Admin only.

The Dashboard shows progress against the annual intake target as a bucket filling toward a target line, a funnel from applicants down to crew where each band's width is the number of people who got that far, and gauges for registration rate, no-show rate and overall conversion. Underneath, median time from induction to crew, CI-card holders, working-visa candidates, screening rate and age bands. Every graphic explains itself on hover. Year and age-band filters apply to everything at once.

Filter by induction session. A session selector sits beside the search box. Choose a session and the pile counts become that cohort's funnel — how many of that group are booked, attended, with EH, or crew. Candidates who were invited but never registered have their own entry.

A Needs attention list. A new pile gathering anyone who needs chasing, across every stage: booked but never showed after their session passed, attended but no details form, sitting with EH without completing setup or signing, or waiting too long anywhere. Each row says why in amber, and those reasons also appear in the normal piles.

Also: With EH and Ready to convert now show ageing labels after 14 days.
