# 5.68.0

*Released 9 October 2026*

You can now see which calls have had their crew list sent, and THE GOAT tells you when a list you sent has gone out of date.

After you create crew lists, THE GOAT asks whether you sent them. Click **Mark as sent** and those calls get a blue edge and ✉ on the Schedule, a "✉ Sent" line in the booking window, and the booking shows "Crew lists: 3 of 5 sent". When every call in a booking is sent, a ✉ appears beside the booking on the Schedule and in All Bookings. The fill colours (full, partial, empty) are unchanged.

If the confirmed crew, the date, the start time or the length of a sent call changes, the marker switches to a dashed edge and the booking window says what changed, for example "Changed since sent: +Smith, −Jones, start 07:00→08:00". Send an updated list and mark it again.

You can now make a crew list for a single call: use the **📋 Crew list** button on any call in the booking window, or tick just the calls you want in Create Crew Lists. Cancelled calls are listed but not ticked. A mark made by mistake can be taken back with **Undo** on the call.
