# 5.25.0

*Released 2 September 2026*

A cancelled call can now be brought back.

Open a cancelled call and choose **Reinstate call** in the cancellation notice. The cancellation is cleared, the name goes back to normal, and the call returns to the Schedule.

**Nobody comes back confirmed.** Anyone who was Confirmed when the call was stood down returns as **Unconfirmed** and has to accept again — they may have been told the call was off and taken other work, and nobody is put back on a shift without agreeing to it. Backups stay backups. The confirm screen lists every person and what they will come back as, before you commit to anything.

As with standing a call down, **no message reaches crew**. THE GOAT lists everyone who needs telling, with their numbers, and says plainly that nothing has been sent. This matters more here than it does on a cancellation: a reinstated call nobody hears about looks staffed in the booking while every one of those people believes they are free.

Reinstating refuses on a call that has already started, and on one that has been locked, invoiced, or had payslips generated — naming which.
