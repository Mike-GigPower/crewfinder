# 5.101.0

*Released 11 November 2026*

Hovering a crew member’s name on Crew → Records and the Inductions grid now shows the first-aid and fork-and-knife icons beside their name when they have emergency information or dietary requirements, so you don’t have to open their record to see it. The icons are for admin and operations only, and aren’t shown on Utilization or Crew Finder.
