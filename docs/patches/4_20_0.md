# 4.20.0

*Released 5 August 2026*

Inductions now visible on a candidate. The expanded panel in Recruitment gains an Inductions section, listing each venue induction a candidate submitted with its completion date and a link to the certificate. Previously this information was captured and pushed to SmartStaff on conversion, but was not visible anywhere in the app beforehand.

Confirmations read at a glance. The candidate's confirmations now render as coloured pills — green for confirmed, amber where something is genuinely outstanding, and grey where an answer is absent but not required. A WWCC acknowledgement is only required from candidates who don't hold a check, so it no longer flags as a gap for those who do.

Clearer criminal-history wording. Reads "Declared" or "None declared" rather than the raw form value.

An induction entry with no venue code recorded is flagged in amber. That entry is also the one convert-to-crew cannot resolve to a SmartStaff venue, so it now surfaces before conversion rather than after.
