# 5.99.0

*Released 10 November 2026*

The Today dashboard is now two: **Bookings & Calls** and **Crew Metrics**.

**Bookings & Calls** has Confirmations to chase, Calls to fill, Crew lists to send, Acknowledgements outstanding, Times outstanding and Bookings at risk. **Crew Metrics** has the Crew Reliability Red Zone, Inductions expiring, Shadowlands, Probation — 144 hours and Recruitment — waiting on us. The tiles themselves haven't changed.

**Counts on the tabs.** Each tab shows the total of its tiles' numbers, so you can see when something is waiting on the other dashboard. The Red Zone isn't included: it counts incidents over the last 30 days, not things to do today, so it's left out to let Crew Metrics read empty when nothing needs doing.

**Moving between them is instant.** Both dashboards load together when you open Today, and switching between them doesn't reload. ⟳ Refresh on either one refreshes both.

**Today remembers where you were.** It opens on the dashboard you used last, and on Bookings & Calls the first time. Links to `#ops` still open Bookings & Calls, `#ops-crew` opens Crew Metrics, and the command palette finds both.
