# 5.15.0

*Released 24 August 2026*

Performance now shows the next 60 days alongside the last 52 weeks.

Forward weeks show what has been booked and what has crew attached, priced through the same rate rules the Estimator uses for quoting. The gap between the two lines is unfilled seats — currently 60 across 12 calls — and the view distinguishes calls where everyone has already been asked from calls nobody has started on yet. Those need opposite responses.

The chart now switches between dollars and hours. In dollars it shows one line, because required crew are charged whether the seat is filled or not — a shortfall is a staffing problem, not revenue at risk. In hours it shows both, because there the gap is real.

Weeks with nothing booked yet are shown as unbooked rather than drawn as zero. The book always thins toward the far edge because calls are entered as they are sold.

Also fixes customer drill-through on the Performance chart, which has not worked since the view was introduced.
