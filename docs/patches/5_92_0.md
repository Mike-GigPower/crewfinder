# 5.92.0

*Released 6 November 2026*

Each call now shows who enters its times, under the call's details: **Crew Boss: name**, **Team Lead: name**, **Crew send own times**, or **Crew Boss call — nobody ticked**. A call with no Crew Boss but two or more Team Leads gets a "No Crew Boss — N Team Leads" warning.

**Crew Boss.** Making someone Crew Boss now also tells SmartStaff, so the Crew Boss can enter the call's times and is the crew's contact. Someone who hasn't accepted a promotion to the call can't be made Crew Boss. If the second half of the save fails, you'll be offered Retry.

**Team Leads.** On a call with a Crew Boss you can now add more than one Team Lead (department leads) and remove one of several. On a call without a Crew Boss there can still be only one: making someone else Team Lead moves it to them.

**Crew send own times.** On a call with no Crew Boss and no Team Lead, Admins get an on/off switch for whether crew can send their own times from Crew Hub. On is the default.

**Linking calls to their Crew Boss call.** In a booking, a call that overlaps a Crew Boss call but isn't linked to it now shows "Not linked to Crew Boss call #…" with a one-click **Link**. All Bookings shows how many such calls each booking has, and the Times outstanding lane shows the same warning. A call can no longer be unlinked if that would leave it with no Crew Boss and two or more Team Leads, and only a Crew Boss call can be chosen as the supervising call.

**Times outstanding and Enter Times.** The lane now says who to chase as Crew Boss, Team Lead or "via Crew Boss call", and marks calls where the crew send their own times or a Crew Boss call has nobody ticked. In Enter Times, times a crew member sent themselves are marked **Self-submitted**, with a strip of any customer timesheet photos (click to enlarge) and notes when there is no photo or the times differ from the schedule by more than 30 minutes.

**Login greetings.** Joe now gets a hello at login, picked at random from a set of clips, with two payday clips added on Tuesdays. Monty and Rich each have a second clip, and every Admin login can also get one of a shared set of greetings, with a Monday-only clip and an early-morning clip from 03:00 to 07:59.
