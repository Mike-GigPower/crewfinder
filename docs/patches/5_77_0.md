# 5.77.0

*Released 20 October 2026*

Late cancellations: crew who were confirmed on a call can now be paid when it is stood down late, and the customer charged, as if it went ahead.

- **Stand down asks two questions:** "Pay confirmed crew for this call?" and "Charge the customer?", each Yes / No with nothing pre-selected. The cut-off for paying crew and for charging the customer can differ, so they are separate. Stand down stays greyed out until both are answered.
- **Times are pre-filled** for confirmed crew when either answer is Yes: the scheduled start, with a 4-hour minimum. Times already entered are never overwritten. The result panel lists who was pre-filled.
- **⏱ Enter Times on a cancelled call:** paid crew show as CANCELLED · PAID and can be edited. Other stood-down crew are greyed out ("Stood down — not paid."). Saving every paid row marks the times checked and takes the call off Ops › Times outstanding, where it shows as Cancelled – paid until then.
- **Booking dialog chips** on a cancelled call: Crew paid / Not paid, Customer charged / Not charged, and Times to check.
- **Pay-only and charge-only reminders:** when only one answer was Yes, an amber note reminds you to zero the other side in SmartStaff before invoicing or the pay run.
- New guide: **Late cancellations**.
