# 4.33.0

*Released 9 August 2026*

Ops tab: Acknowledgements outstanding

A third lane showing crew who haven't answered since a call's timing changed or since being promoted off standby — what kind of question is outstanding, how soon the call is, and what the timing used to be. Ops can dismiss a timing re-confirm they've already settled by phone.

Also: the Ops tab now scrolls with multiple lanes, and call dates sit beside the name on every card.
