# 4.6.0

*Released 17 July 2026*

# THE GOAT v4.6.0 — Work eligibility & visa in Recruitment

*Ops can now see an applicant's right-to-work status and, for visa holders, verify it before converting. The data capture and AI visa scan are already live in the background; this brings the ops-facing side into THE GOAT.*

## What's new

- **Work eligibility on every applicant.** The Recruitment detail now shows each applicant's declared status — **Australian Citizen / Permanent Resident** or **Working visa** — and a **Working visa** chip appears on the list so they're easy to spot.

- **Visa details for admins.** For a working-visa applicant, **admins** can see their passport number, country of issue, a **View visa** link to the uploaded document, and the visa's AI-scanned facts — subclass, expiry, conditions — shown under a clear **"AI-suggested — verify via VEVO"** heading, so they're a starting point, never the final word. The applicant's VEVO consent and date show too. These sensitive details are **admin-only**, the same as health information.

- **Record the check.** Once an admin has confirmed work rights via VEVO, **Mark VEVO verified** records it against the applicant and clears the reminder for everyone.

- **A reminder before converting a visa holder.** Converting a working-visa applicant who hasn't been VEVO-verified shows a non-blocking prompt — *"Work rights haven't been VEVO-verified by an admin yet — proceed anyway?"* You stay in control; it's just a conscious check (employing someone without work rights is an offence, so it's a deliberate safeguard).

- **Open GOAT tabs in separate windows.** Click the **⧉ New window** button on the right of the tab bar to pop the current tab out — so you can keep Schedule open in one window and Crew Finder in another. The address bar now reflects which tab you're on, so tabs can be bookmarked and shared, and tab access still follows your normal permissions.
