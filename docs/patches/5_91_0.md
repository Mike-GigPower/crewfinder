# 5.91.0

*Released 2 November 2026*

Times in THE GOAT now always use the 24-hour clock, with no am or pm. The time boxes for adding unavailability (Your shifts and a crew record) and for an induction session's start, end and booking-closes are now 24-hour boxes: type 1730 or 17:30, and a time that can't be read is outlined in red and isn't saved. Induction session times, set-by and cancelled-at lines, and other date-and-time stamps now show 24-hour times, and times just after midnight show as 00:05 rather than 24:05. And when a new version is available, clicking Download update now plays one of five voice clips at random.
