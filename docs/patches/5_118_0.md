# 5.118.0

*Released 22 November 2026*

Import from check-in now applies the 4-hour minimum, and it goes by how long the call was booked for in SmartStaff.

A call booked for 4 hours or more is topped up when someone's paid time is under 4 hours. Their finish is written as the call's start plus 4 hours, the way it was typed on the Google Sheet. The review shows both times, for example 22:00–00:30 → 02:00, with the note "4-hour minimum applied". These rows stay ticked.

A call booked for under 4 hours is paid as worked, with no minimum. The review says so, for example "booked for 3 h — paid as worked".

A late start is topped to that same finish. Someone marked Late, or with a typed start after the call time, is written to the call's start plus 4 hours, not to 4 hours from when they arrived. If they worked past that, their own finish stands.

A call with no booked length is held for review. Anyone on it with under 4 hours is flagged in amber and left unticked, and nothing is added to their time. The same happens to any row THE GOAT can't work the minimum out for.
