# 5.125.0

*Released 26 November 2026*

A crew member the Crew Boss added on the day on the check-in sheet now shows up when you import times from check-in. You can book them onto the call and write their times in one step.

On Crew Hub's check-in sheet, the Crew Boss can add someone who isn't booked on the call, either moved from another call in the same booking or called in on short notice. They can also say who the person is covering for.

In Import from check-in, these people are listed under the call as added on the day, with the reason, for example "moved from 10:00 Bump In · covering Jane Smith". They start unticked. Tick a row to book that person onto the call and write their times. Someone with no sign-on and sign-off can't be ticked.

When a moved person is written, their place on the call they came from is set to a new status, Moved. Moved isn't paid and doesn't count as a clash. To undo a move, set them back to Confirmed. Booking someone this way sends them no SMS or email.
