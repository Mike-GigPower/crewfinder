# 5.5.2

*Released 18 August 2026*

Crew Finder could report a crew member as expired at a multi-venue induction when a current record existed at another venue under the same umbrella. Someone inducted at MCG - Gate 7 but lapsed at another MCG gate read as Expired, even though the catalogue treats all MCG gates as one induction.

Status is now taken across every venue the induction covers, not the first record that happens to match. Twelve crew across MCG and MCEC are corrected. Genuine expiries are unaffected — 88 crew still read Expired at MCG.

Also corrects the same check in ASK THE GOAT, which shares the underlying logic.
