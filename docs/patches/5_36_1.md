# 5.36.1

*Released 9 September 2026*

Fixes a bug where crew members created through the automated Applicant → Crew Member conversion, or through Administration → Add Crew Member, were saved with no pay rate at all. They now start on T1.

Because the rate was missing rather than wrong, SmartStaff displayed the first entry in the rate list instead, so the records looked correctly rated. Every call those crew worked then needed a rate picked by hand, and on at least one call the wrong one was chosen.

Also fixes the SmartStaff Add Crew page, which was blanking pay-rate and tax fields whenever a caller didn't submit them.

The 17 affected crew and their 41 upcoming bookings have been corrected in the database separately.
