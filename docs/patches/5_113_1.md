# 5.113.1

*Released 20 November 2026*

Crew Finder shows induction badges again for calls at Crown Live and Crown Palladium.

Crown Live is the venue that used to be called Crown Melbourne - Palms. After it was renamed in SmartStaff, Crew Finder no longer recognised it as a Crown venue, so calls there showed no induction badges and inducted crew were not listed first. Crown Live and Crown Palladium are now checked against the Crown induction, the same as Crown Melbourne.

Crown venues that do not need an induction, such as Crown Metropol and Crown Riverwalk, are unchanged.
