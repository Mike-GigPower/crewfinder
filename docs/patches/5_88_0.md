# 5.88.0

*Released 27 October 2026*

**Staffing plans now look at what each call is for.** A licence named in a call's Notes (for example Forklift, EWP or WWCC) is applied to every place, or to at least N places for "2x Forklift". When only the call name or Call Type hints at a licence, the plan asks you first and builds nothing until you answer. Scarce licence holders go first to calls where every place needs the licence. On LX, VX, SX, Backline and Spot calls, crew who have worked that department in the last 3 years rank first. Wardrobe and Seamstress calls are planned only from those groups, and Wardrobe-only crew aren't put on general calls unless nobody else is available. Plans say clearly when places are left empty and why.
