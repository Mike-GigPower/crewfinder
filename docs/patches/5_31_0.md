# 5.31.0

*Released 4 September 2026*

Attach a visa document and THE GOAT reads it.

Manage Crew → Visa now takes two documents: the IMMI grant notification and the
VEVO Visa Details Check. Attaching either one reads it and fills the form —
subclass, grant number, TRN, passport number and country, grant and expiry
dates, and the visa conditions — for you to check before saving. Nothing is
written until you press Save.

The VEVO check is now stored on the record beside the visa document, and it is
what sets the work limitation and the verification stamp. The stamp comes from
the document's own "valid as at" timestamp rather than being typed, and it is
saved together with the document that supports it.

Two safeguards worth knowing. The name on the document is shown and checked
against the crew member, so a document dropped on the wrong record says so. And
where the grant notification and the VEVO check disagree, VEVO wins and the
disagreement is shown — a grant letter records what was granted and cannot know
about a later variation or cancellation.

Anything read from a document is marked as unverified until you save it, and any
field the reader couldn't find is named rather than left silently blank.
