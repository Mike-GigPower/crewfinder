# 5.32.0

*Released 7 September 2026*

New crew members are now emailed their Crew Hub login automatically.

When an applicant is converted to crew, they receive a branded welcome email
with their EIN (their Crew Hub username), the temporary password and a link to
crew.gigpower.com — the email ops previously typed out by hand. The convert
dialog reports whether it sent, was blocked or failed, so nobody has to assume.

Converted crew rows carry a "Resend welcome email" button for the cases the
automatic send can't cover — it was blocked at the time, or the crew member
never found it.

The automatic send happens once per crew member: re-running a conversion to
retry a licence or induction push will not send a second copy of someone's
login. A deliberate resend is limited to one per day.

Requires the recruitment-welcome edge function and the induction_emails_sent
'welcome' kind, both deployed 7 September 2026.
