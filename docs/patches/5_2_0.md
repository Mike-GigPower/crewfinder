# 5.2.0

*Released 13 August 2026*

Payslips on the crew record. Open any crew member in Manage Crew and there's a new Payslips tab alongside Inductions and Licences — every pay week they have on record, newest first, with the workslip PDF one click away. Same list the crew member sees in Crew Hub, so you're both looking at the same thing when they call.

Admin only, including operations users who have elevated.

Access logging. Opening a payslip PDF is recorded — who opened it, whose payslip, which week. Viewing the list of weeks isn't recorded; only opening a PDF is.

Also in this release: the navigation wordmark no longer wraps at narrow window widths.
