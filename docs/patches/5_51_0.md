# 5.51.0

*Released 17 September 2026*

Crew Finder now lists crew who hold a current venue induction first.

Search a call at a venue that requires an induction and the available list leads with the people who are current for it, highest rating first. Everyone else follows, also by rating. Crew whose induction is expiring soon sit between the two — still valid on the day, but worth knowing before you book them.

Nobody is hidden and nobody is blocked. Same crew, same counts, same badges. A missing or expired induction has never stopped someone being booked and still doesn't; it now costs them their place at the top of the list instead of only carrying a warning.

Searching several calls at once, someone current at both venues sorts above someone current at one, who sorts above someone current at neither.

Venues that don't require an induction are unaffected — the order there is exactly what it was.

Clicking a column header still sorts the whole list by that column, as before.
