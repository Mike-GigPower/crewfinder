# 5.96.0

*Released 9 November 2026*

New on the call dialog and in **Enter Times**: **‹ Prev** and **Next ›** to step between a booking's calls.

The buttons sit in the header and follow the booking's calls in start date and time order. You stay on the same view: the call dialog keeps its tab and its Booked Crew filter and order, and Enter Times opens the next call's times. The header shows where you are (for example "3 of 7"), and hovering a button names the call it goes to.

**✓ Accept & Next ›** in Enter Times accepts the times and opens the next call's times in one click. It only moves on if the save and the accept both succeed. Next on its own never saves or accepts anything.

Enter Times now asks before throwing away times you have typed and not saved: **Stay**, **Discard**, or **Save and continue**. It asks whether you leave with Prev, Next, Back, ✕, Esc, Close or a breadcrumb. Save and continue saves (it doesn't accept), and only moves on if the save worked.

Enter Times also names the call, with its date and start time, under the title.

Back still goes where it did before: stepping between calls doesn't add to the trail.
