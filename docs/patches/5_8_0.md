# 5.8.0

*Released 20 August 2026*

Ops can now resend a candidate's details-form or contract-signing link from the applicant row in Recruitment.

These two emails previously re-fired whenever a candidate's status was bounced. They now go through the same guarded sender as every other recruitment email, which adds an audit record of what was sent and prevents accidental duplicates — so the resend is a deliberate button rather than a side effect. It's limited to once per candidate per day; a second click the same day reports "already resent today" rather than sending again.

The Resend contract button appears only once a commencement date is set, since the contract isn't signable without one.

Also fixed: the Recruitment Dashboard and Sessions views now scroll instead of clipping at the bottom of the window — the same problem fixed for the applicant list in v5.7.2, in the two views that fix didn't cover.
