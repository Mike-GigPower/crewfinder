# 4.14.0

*Released 29 July 2026*

Recommended feeds now show up properly in the Crew Finder.

Until now, selecting a call also selected everything it fed — including calls linked by a recommended feed. That meant anyone with a clash on a recommended call was pushed down the list even though you weren't trying to fill that call.

Recommended calls are now considered rather than searched. They show with a dotted purple edge, and they don't count towards the calls you're filling. Crew who clash one still appear, ranked by how many of the whole set they're free for:

ALL 2 CALLS · Available for 2 of 3

The first line is the calls you're actually filling. The second counts the recommended call too, and drives the order — people free for everything come first.

Recommended feeds are now purple, not gold, so they're distinguishable at a glance:

↓ feeds / ↑ fed by — gold and grey, solid. A locked feed: crew are committed to both calls.
↓ suggests / ↑ suggested by — purple, dashed. A recommended feed: crew are preferred, but answer each call separately.

In a crew member's availability breakdown, a recommended call is marked with ~ before the call number. If they clash it, it still shows red — a clash is a clash regardless of feed type.

Also fixed: the over-subscription warning now appears on calls fed only by recommended feeds, which previously showed nothing.

Nothing changes for locked feeds. Every existing feed is locked unless someone has deliberately made it recommended.
