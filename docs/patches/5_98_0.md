# 5.98.0

*Released 10 November 2026*

Crew → Records has a new Last shift column, and THE GOAT is ready for Crew Hub's offer reminders. The Last shift column was in 5.97.0, which never went out, so it arrives with this version.

**Last shift.** The new column sits between Reliability 12 mo and Push and shows the date of each crew member's most recent worked shift. A worked shift is a confirmed shift dated today or earlier, the same rule as the Hours tab. Cancelled calls, no-shows and future shifts don't count. Hover the date to see the booking, call and start time; click it to open that call. Dates more than 12 months ago show in amber, and "Never" means no confirmed shift on record. The new "No shift in 12 months" filter shows crew whose last shift was over 12 months ago, including those who have never had one. The column sorts, and Export CSV gains Last shift date and Last shift columns.

**Offer reminders: three new statuses.** Crew Hub will remind crew about shift offers they haven't answered, and withdraw an offer that stays unanswered. A call's Booked Crew list shows those withdrawn offers with their own status and filter chip:

- **No response** (red): no answer 30 hours after the offer, and a notification did reach their phone. This counts against the crew member.
- **Not delivered** (grey): no answer after 30 hours, and no notification ever reached them. This doesn't count against them.
- **Withdrawn** (grey): a short-notice offer still unanswered when the call started. This doesn't count against them.

**Offering the shift again.** Change someone at No response to Unconfirmed and THE GOAT asks whether to send them a new offer, the same as for Declined. Not delivered and Withdrawn change like any other status and don't send an offer. Someone offered again after their offer was withdrawn shows a "2nd offer" tag (then "3rd offer", and so on) until they answer.

**Records.** Reliability 12 mo now shows No resp after Late and No-show: how many offers were withdrawn for no response in the last 12 months. Hover for the all-time figure. Sorting by Reliability puts no-shows first, then lates, then No response. Export CSV gains No response 12 mo and No response all time.

**Elsewhere.** Crew Finder treats a withdrawn offer as declined, so "Declined this call" hides it. Stand down and crew lists leave these crew out, as they do for Declined.

**For Ops:** reminders and automatic withdrawal start on go-live day, which will be announced separately. Until then none of these statuses will appear.
