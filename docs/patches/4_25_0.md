# 4.25.0

*Released 6 August 2026*

The Schedule range now goes up to 90 days, for planning further ahead than the previous 28-day ceiling. Booking and call names stay pinned to the left as the dates scroll.

Two smaller fixes: the "Warnings:" label now mutes to grey once every warning beneath it has been acknowledged, instead of staying orange and reading as unresolved. And a clash chip now carries a ⚡ to match its badge, rather than sharing the ⚠ used for warnings.
