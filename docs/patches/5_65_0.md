# 5.65.0

*Released 7 October 2026*

Backups can now be called out. When a call is short and has crew on standby, the call dialog shows **Call out backups (N)**. It lists who will be asked, anyone skipped and why, and how many places are open. **Send call-out** offers the place to all of them at once by push, and the first to accept in Crew Hub gets it. While it's open, a strip under the Crew line shows how many were asked, confirmed and declined, with a **Close call-out** button, and called-out crew carry a "called out" tag. When the places fill or the call starts, the call-out closes and anyone who didn't answer goes back on standby. Crew Finder and the Schedule show "on standby" and "Call-out open".

Promote is now link-safe. Promoting someone on a call that feeds a later call, such as a load-in leading to a load-out, moves them off standby on those calls too, as one promotion they answer once in Crew Hub. If they don't hold a place on a later call, Promote is refused and names the calls to fix first. This has been live on 5.64.0 since 7 October, because the server enforces it. On 5.64.0 a refused Promote shows a raw error message instead of naming the calls; 5.65.0 shows the plain explanation.

Unanswered promotions no longer count as filled. Finder, the Today lane and its calendar, the Schedule and the call dialog show them as "awaiting reply" until the crew member answers.
