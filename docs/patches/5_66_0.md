# 5.66.0

*Released 7 October 2026*

Crew records have a new Hours tab. It shows each person's recorded hours (the times keyed after each shift, minus breaks) beside the hours they were scheduled for, how much their shifts ran over and finished early, and how much was a 4-hour minimum top-up on a short shift. A chart breaks the hours down by week, month or year, and a second chart tracks the count toward the 144-hour probation and marks the date it was reached. The count restarts after a gap of 18 months or more between shifts, and the tab says when that has happened. **All shifts** lists every shift with its scheduled and recorded hours and the running count.

Today › Operations has a new **Probation — 144 hours** tile for admins. The badge counts crew who reached 144 hours in the last 30 days, so their base pay rate can be changed in Employment Hero. The list behind it also shows crew at 120 hours or more who are approaching it. Clicking a name opens their Hours tab. The list is worked out at most every 3 hours; **Recalculate now** refreshes it on the spot, which takes about 10 seconds.
