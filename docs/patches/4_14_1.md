# 4.14.1

*Released 30 July 2026*

Crew Lists now default to the Phone column and Confirmed crew only.

Schedule flags over-booked calls in red. A call with more crew booked than
required previously showed green as though it were correctly filled, and is
now counted separately in the booking summary.

Generated timesheets no longer show HTML entities in names. Apostrophe
surnames such as O'Brien were written into the sheet as O&#39;Brien. Fixed
for both the Google Sheet and the .xlsx download.
