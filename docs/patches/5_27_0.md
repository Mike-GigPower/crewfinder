# 5.27.0

*Released 2 September 2026*

Ops can now see how a call was filled — who was offered it, who accepted, and when.

Open any call and choose **📋 Responses**. Each crew member's row shows when they accepted and how long they took, what else they are holding on the same booking, how much work they have had in the last 90 days, and their record of declines, no-shows and cancellations. Every column sorts, so the roster can be read whichever way the question runs.

**It lays out the facts; it does not rank anyone.** There is no score and no recommendation — deciding who comes off a call is a judgement, and this is the information to make it with.

Three things the screen says about itself, because each one changes how a number should be read. Response tracking began 27 July 2026, so thin history means little was measured rather than that someone is unreliable. Crew removed from a call earlier cannot appear, because removing someone deletes the record. And a shift entered by phone or by ops carries no response time — that is recorded, not missing.

Also in this release: a cancellation no longer counts against a crew member anywhere. Standing someone down is something we did, not something they chose, and the Crew Responsiveness page now classifies it by what they were before we cancelled — so accepting everything we offer can never make someone look less reliable.
