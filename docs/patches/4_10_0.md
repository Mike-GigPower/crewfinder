# 4.10.0

*Released 20 July 2026*

## Timing changes now ask crew to re-confirm

Edit a call's start, date or length after crew are on it and confirmed crew are
asked to re-confirm rather than being silently moved. Their shift card shows the
change — "was Fri 6:00am, now Fri 8:00am" — with Accept or Decline. A decline
opens the gap straight away so it can be refilled.

Standby crew get a lighter version: the same detail with a single "No longer
available" opt-out, so staying silent keeps them on standby. Crew still deciding
on an offer see the new timing update on their card automatically.

**New in the app:** the crew roster in the call dialog now shows an
**⏳ awaiting re-confirm** badge against anyone with an outstanding change — so
you can see at a glance who hasn't answered on a re-timed call.

Non-timing edits — call name, crew required, notes, booking contact — go out as
an informational update and need no answer.

## Crew Utilisation — see the shift behind the cell

Hover any green day cell on the Crew Utilisation page to see what that person is
actually on: venue, booking, call name and times. No more cross-referencing the
schedule to find out why someone is unavailable.

## Who do I call — crew boss setup

Crew boss for a call can now be set from the booking dialog in THE GOAT, rather
than only in SmartStaff. The schedule also warns you when a change would leave

Crew already see their contact on every shift, offer and standby card in Crew
