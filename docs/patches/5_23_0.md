# 5.23.0

*Released 31 August 2026*

Working-visa crew now have a visible home in THE GOAT.

Administration → Compliance → Visa Register lists everyone holding a working
visa, soonest expiry first, with filters for expired, expiring within 90 days,
never VEVO-checked, not on the roster, and no expiry recorded.

Manage Crew gains a Visa tab for recording and editing one person's visa —
subclass, grant and passport numbers, work rights expiry, work limitation,
conditions and the visa document — plus a VEVO work-rights check that records
who verified it and when.

An expired visa is deliberately kept separate from licence expiry. A lapsed
licence is something to chase; a lapsed visa means no right to work, so it gets
its own badge and its own words rather than joining the licence compliance pill.

Both surfaces are ADMIN ONLY. Passport numbers, grant numbers and visa
conditions are immigration PII and the Visa tab does not appear for Operations,
Leadership or Crew — nothing changes for them in this release.

Also fixes a latent hazard in admin-set-visa.php, which replaced every column on
every write. Recording a VEVO check would have blanked the passport number,
subclass and expiry in the same statement.
