# 5.93.1

*Released 6 November 2026*

Phone numbers are now saved and shown in the standard format, for example **0411 538 575** or **(03) 9376 9500**, instead of run together as 0411538575.

THE GOAT now formats the number whenever it saves one to SmartStaff: adding or converting crew, updating an existing crew member on convert, editing a crew record, and adding or editing contacts and customers. Numbers it can't confidently read as Australian, such as overseas numbers, are saved exactly as typed.

Phone numbers in Crew Finder, Forecast, Crew Records and contact lists now display in the same format. Tapping a number still dials it as before.
