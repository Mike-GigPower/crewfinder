# 5.70.0

*Released 9 October 2026*

THE GOAT now warns you before a call it feeds goes over its number. When crew are added to a load-in, the load out it feeds gets them too, even when it is already full, so a load out could quietly end up 21 of 20.

You'll see the warning when you add crew in the Crew Finder (Add, Add & Confirm or Send SMS, and through ASK THE GOAT), when you Promote a backup, and in the Call out backups preview. It names the call and the numbers, for example *Load Out is already full: 20 of 20 places taken … which will make it 21 of 20*. Choose **Continue anyway** or **Cancel**. Nothing is saved until you choose. Recommended feeds never warn, because crew answer those separately.

Removing someone with **✕** now checks the calls linked to this one. If they're also on the load out (or the load-in), you can choose **Remove from both**, **this call only**, or **Cancel**, so a load out no longer keeps a place for someone who has come off its load-in.
