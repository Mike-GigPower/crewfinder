# 5.74.2

*Released 15 October 2026*

Google Sheet timesheets no longer list promoted backups who haven't accepted yet.

- **Generate Google Sheet:** anyone promoted off backup who hasn't accepted is left off their call's tab, so the crew boss can't time someone who may not be working.
- **You're told who:** the "Google Sheet created" window names anyone left off, and the call they were left off.
- **If they accept and work:** the boss adds them to the sheet by EIN as normal, and Import Times writes their times.
