# 4.38.0

*Released 12 August 2026*

Induction sessions. The Recruitment tab has a new Sessions view: create induction sittings with a date, time, venue, capacity and meeting details, open them for booking, and see how many have booked and how many are waitlisted. A seat-supply line shows how many places remain across all open sessions. Rosters list who's booked with their contact details, and ops can book or cancel on someone's behalf — including deliberately overfilling a session when needed.

"None of these suit me." Candidates who can't make any offered session can say so and give a reason, and they're grouped by reason so you can see whether another sitting is needed.

The recruitment dashboard now excludes test records. It will read as empty until real candidates start moving through the pipeline — that's correct, and every number from here on is real.

The subheading under Recruitment now describes whichever sub-view you're looking at.
