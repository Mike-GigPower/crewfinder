# 5.5.1

*Released 17 August 2026*

Licence triage now captures the date of attainment from the card alongside the expiry, and carries it onto every row when a card is split into multiple licences.

The evidence pane opens fitted to width, "Can't tell" is relabelled "Not in catalogue", and Escape no longer closes the screen from inside a date field.
