# 5.20.0

*Released 27 August 2026*

A new lane on Today shows calls where crew times are still outstanding — who's owed, how many, and who to chase. Rows split into times submitted and awaiting review, times not submitted yet, and calls with no crew boss at all, so the ones that will never arrive on their own stand out.

Opening a row goes straight to Enter Times with the crew boss's submitted figures already filled in and marked, so you can check them rather than type them. Accepting ticks the call as times filled.

Also fixes lane tiles overlapping on smaller laptop screens.
