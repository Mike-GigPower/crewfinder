# 5.59.0

*Released 23 September 2026*

Crew records and calls now link to each other, with a breadcrumb trail to find your way back. On a crew member's Calls tab, click a call to open it. In a call, click a crew member's name to open their record.

A trail above the dialog title now shows where you came from, and every step in it is clickable. It works across bookings, calls, Enter Times and the Visa and Licence registers, which now keep their filter when you open someone from them and come back. The call dialog also names its booking under the title.

If you have typed changes into a crew member's Details and try to leave, THE GOAT now asks whether to save or discard them. Saving a crew record you reached from a call keeps it open and shows Saved.
