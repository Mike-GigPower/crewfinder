# 5.74.3

*Released 16 October 2026*

Estimator exports made on or after 14 October import again.

- **Create Booking from an estimate:** exports from the updated Estimator (schema 1.2) were refused with "Unsupported schema version". They now import as before.
- **MOPT quotes:** Training and Field of Play calls come through under those names.
