# 5.111.0

*Released 18 November 2026*

Accounting › Invoicing now lists SmartStaff invoices (read-only, Mike only for now).

- **Invoices:** newest first, 50 a page, with each invoice's booking, customer, number of calls, total ex and inc GST, and whether it has been exported to Xero.
- **Search** by invoice number, booking name or customer name. Names with an ampersand are found.
- **Open an invoice** to see its lines, laid out like the PDF, its GST and total, and the calls it covers. Booking and call links open their dialogs, and Back returns to the invoice.
- **📄 PDF** opens SmartStaff's own invoice PDF.
- Totals are worked out the way the PDF works them out, so they match it to the cent.
- Amber flags mark an invoice whose Xero export would count a flat-rate line differently from the PDF, an invoice with no lines, and a call that is on an invoice but has no invoice line.
- Nothing on this screen changes an invoice. Generate, edit, email and export are still done in SmartStaff.
