# 4.19.0

*Released 5 August 2026*

Promoting a backup into a confirmed spot now asks the crew member to confirm rather than simply booking them. They accepted standby, sometimes weeks earlier, and their circumstances may have changed.

Crew Hub shows them Accept / Can't make it. Declining removes the shift and clears their calendar.

In the call dialog, anyone promoted but not yet confirmed carries an amber ↑ promoted — not confirmed pill. If you've already spoken to them by phone, click it to dismiss — that records the confirmation as coming from ops rather than the crew member.
