# 4.7.0

*Released 17 July 2026*

THE GOAT v4.7.0 — Contract & visa carried into SmartStaff on convert

When you convert an applicant to crew, their signed contract — and, for visa holders, their visa document and details — now land on the SmartStaff crew record. The visible change is small (the convert results list a couple more lines), but the crew record is now complete.

What's new


Converting to crew now stores the signed contract on the crew member's record. The signed employment contract PDF (with its signed date and version) is saved to SmartStaff as part of Convert to Crew — so the signed document lives with the crew member, alongside their licences and inductions, instead of only in the onboarding system.
Working-visa crew — visa document + details carried across too. For an applicant on a working visa, converting them also stores their visa PDF and details: passport number, country, subclass, grant/expiry dates, conditions, and the recorded VEVO check. They're flagged as a visa worker on the crew record so the visa can be tracked.
The convert results now show Contract and Visa lines — ✓ added / • already on file / ✗ failed — next to the existing licence and induction outcomes, so you can see exactly what carried across. As always, a single item failing is flagged but never blocks the conversion.


Under the hood

New SmartStaff storage — a per-crew documents table, a visa table, and a visa-worker flag on the crew record — receives the contract and visa from THE GOAT at convert time. Visa/passport details are held as admin-only immigration PII.
