# 5.116.0

*Released 20 November 2026*

Today now has a **System** tab, visible to Admin logins only. It answers the question nobody could answer before: when the GOAT feels slow, what is actually slow?

Four gauges across the top show how long each back end is taking right now — SmartStaff, Supabase, the Crew Hub, and everything else. Under them, a range strip and a sixty-minute trace show SmartStaff's typical speed, its worst moments, and when the background crew-cache refresh ran. A row of lamps shows how many SmartStaff requests are queued at that moment. Everything updates every five seconds while the tab is open, and stops when it isn't.

Below the graphics: how often the app checks your SmartStaff session is still alive, how often it has had to log you back in silently and why, when the crew cache last refreshed, and the twenty slowest requests in the last hour.

**Test now** makes three small calls to SmartStaff, one after another, and shows how long each took — a quick way to tell a slow SmartStaff from a slow network.

**If it feels slow, open Today → System and press Copy summary, or Reveal log, and send that to Mike.** The log lives in `~/Library/Logs/The GOAT/health.log` and keeps the last few megabytes. It records how long requests took and whether they failed — never what was in them, and never anything about crew.

**Nothing about how the GOAT talks to SmartStaff has changed in this release.** No timeout, no session check, no refresh timing and no behaviour has been altered. This release only measures. The numbers it collects are what the next one will use to make things faster.
