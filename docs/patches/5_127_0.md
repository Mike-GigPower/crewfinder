# 5.127.0

*Released 27 November 2026*

Confirmed crew can no longer decline a time change in Crew Hub — they now just see it, and anyone who can't make the new time calls the office. The call dialog, Schedule and Ops lane now show who hasn't seen a time change yet, and the Responses tab shows "saw new time" when they have. Backups still answer a time change with No longer available.
