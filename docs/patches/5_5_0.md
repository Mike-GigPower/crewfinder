# 5.5.0

*Released 17 August 2026*

Cancelling a session now emails everyone booked. The Cancel control has been added to the Sessions view — it reverts every booking, clears the waitlist, and emails each affected candidate with a link to pick another session. The confirmation tells you how many people will be emailed before you commit to it.

Inviting a candidate now asks which intake they're being invited to — a picklist of existing intakes rather than free text. This is what lets a candidate see the right sessions when they open their booking link.

Editing a session tells you what actually happened. The old "they will NOT be notified" warning is gone, because they now are. Saves report what was sent, including anything blocked, so silence is never mistaken for success.

Sessions can now carry a street address and an entry-point map for the venue. Nothing displays them yet, but they can be filled in now. "Meeting notes" is renamed to "What attendees need to know" — that's where entry instructions belong.

Reminders can be sent manually for a session, at three days out or the morning of.
