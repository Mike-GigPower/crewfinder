# 5.12.0

*Released 23 August 2026*

Ops can now add notes against a candidate during recruitment, and tick individual notes to carry across to the crew record when the candidate is converted.

Notes thread with an always-visible composer in the expanded candidate row, showing author and date
A note count and age indicator on the collapsed row
Notes are append-only; carrying to the crew record is opt-in per note
The convert preview shows the exact text that will land on the crew record

Also in this release: apostrophes in crew names, addresses and notes are no longer stored as &#39;, and 268 existing records have been corrected — crew like O'Brien are now findable by their real surname.
