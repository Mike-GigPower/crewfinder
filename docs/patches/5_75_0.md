# 5.75.0

*Released 16 October 2026*

Ask the GOAT can now answer licence questions: who on a call is missing a white card, forklift ticket or any other licence, and what licences a crew member holds.

- **Who on a call is missing a licence:** ask "who on call 37887 doesn't have a white card?" or "anyone on Saturday's load out without a forklift ticket?". The GOAT names everyone with no such licence on file, anyone who holds it but has no expiry date recorded, anyone whose licence has expired or is about to by the day of the call, and anyone with an uncoded licence that looks like the right card (worth checking). Works for any licence - white card (CI), forklift, EWP, WWCC, first aid, driver classes and the rest. A heavier driver class counts for a lighter one.
- **Finding the call:** give a call number, a booking number, or a booking name and day. Name a whole booking and every call on it is checked.
- **A crew member's licences:** ask "what licences does Jack Smith hold?" or "when does EIN 6070's forklift expire?". If a few people share the name, each is listed.
- Declined, stood-down and no-show crew are not counted. "Holds" means a licence coded that way is on file - not proof the card itself is right.
- Replying to a question the GOAT has just asked you (for example "all of them") no longer gets the REALLY? goat.
