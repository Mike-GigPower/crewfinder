# 5.14.1

*Released 24 August 2026*

The "delivered, not yet invoiced" band on the Performance view now splits hours at the day/night boundary and prices each part at its own rate, instead of applying the day rate throughout.

On current data that band runs 37% night hours, so the estimate was materially low — one recent week rose 18%. The band was always an estimate and still is; this makes it a closer one.
