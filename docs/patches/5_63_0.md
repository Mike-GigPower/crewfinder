# 5.63.0

*Released 24 September 2026*

On Today, Operations, the Confirmations to chase card now answers the two questions that matter when chasing an offer: how soon the call starts, and whether the offer actually reached the crew member's phone.

The old Status chart is gone. Since SMS was retired it showed every offer as "Not messaged" in red, even when the offer had been pushed to the person's phone. It is replaced by Delivery: Delivered, No device (they have no Crew Hub notifications, so ring them), Not attempted (a system fault - tell Mike), Send failed, and Before tracking for offers made before 20 September. These are the same figures the Crew Hub dashboard shows.

The Age chart is replaced by Call starts in, using the same under 48 hours, 2 to 7 days and over 7 days groups as the other Ops cards. Open the card to see the list sorted with the soonest call first. Each person shows their delivery status, so clicking under 48 hours and then No device gives you exactly who to ring today.
