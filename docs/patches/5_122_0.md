# 5.122.0

*Released 25 November 2026*

A conversion that half-worked can now be finished with one click.

When you convert a candidate, THE GOAT pushes their licences, inductions, signed contract and dietary answer across to SmartStaff. Those pushes are deliberately best-effort — the crew member is created whether or not they land — but until now a failed push could only be repaired by hand, and for the contract that meant finding a PDF you don't have. Re-running Convert to crew never retried them: it refuses once the candidate is marked Active crew.

**Finish setup** now does it. Every Active crew row has the button (Admins only), and it re-runs exactly those four pushes. It is safe to press at any time, as often as you like: anything already in SmartStaff comes back **already on file** rather than being added twice or overwritten. A dietary record the crew member entered in Crew Hub, or you entered on the Dietary tab, is checked first and left alone.

The convert result screen offers the same thing while you are still looking at it. If a step fails, **Retry failed steps** appears next to Done, fills in what it can and redraws the list in place. It disappears once everything reads ✓ or already on file. Visas are the exception and the screen now says so — a failed visa push is fixed on the crew record, not by Retry.

SmartStaff errors are in plain English. Instead of a line of Python, a step that couldn't get through now reads *Couldn't reach SmartStaff (connection timed out) — nothing was saved for this step*, or, when SmartStaff answered too slowly, *SmartStaff didn't answer in time — this step may or may not have saved*. The technical detail is still there behind a small **details** link. And when SmartStaff can't be reached at all, Finish setup says so in about ten seconds rather than working through four timeouts one after another.

A failed induction push is no longer silent. Until now, if SmartStaff couldn't be reached part-way through a conversion, the result screen left the Inductions line out altogether — so the inductions hadn't gone across and nobody was told. That is how two of them went missing on 24 November. Any step that can't be pushed now shows as a failure with **Retry failed steps** beside it, whatever went wrong on the way. If someone was converted while SmartStaff was playing up over the last few weeks, **Finish setup** on their Active crew row will fill in whatever was missed; anything showing **added** rather than already on file was one of these gaps.

Dietary now reports as its own line on the result screen — **added**, **already on file** or a failure — alongside Licences, Inductions and Contract, instead of a separate warning box.
