# 5.84.0

*Released 22 October 2026*

Admins can now update a crew member's photo from inside THE GOAT. Choose or drag in a JPEG or PNG, crop it in a passport-style frame, and save.

- **Where:** click the photo on a crew record (hover shows "📷 Update"), use the Replace link beside Missing or Low-res people in the accreditation photo export results, or click the new Photo column on Crew → Records.
- **Cropping:** drag to position and use the zoom slider. The frame is fixed at passport shape (35:45) and the image always fills it. Two previews show the full-size passport photo and the SmartStaff thumbnail exactly as they'll be saved.
- **What's saved:** the full-size passport photo goes to the Gig Power photo store, which the accreditation export now uses first. A thumbnail-shaped copy replaces the SmartStaff profile photo, and SmartStaff keeps a backup of the old one. The new photo shows straight away everywhere in THE GOAT.
- **If SmartStaff doesn't take the thumbnail:** the full-size photo is still saved, and the dialog says so with a Retry thumbnail button.
- **Records → Photo column:** Full-size · date, Low-res · date (SmartStaff photo only), or None. Amber when the photo is more than 2 years old. It sorts None first, then oldest to newest, and is included in Export CSV.
- Only JPEG and PNG files up to 15 MB are accepted, and a crop narrower than 200 pixels is refused as too small for accreditation. There is no delete or undo, and the uncropped original isn't kept.
- Admins only.
