# 5.110.0

*Released 18 November 2026*

Change pay grade now updates Employment Hero as well as SmartStaff.

- **What changes in Employment Hero:** the Pay Rate Template, the Primary Pay Category and the Level tag (Level 1, Level 2 or Level 3) move to match the new grade. Other tags such as In-Active and Management are left as they are, and the pay rate follows the new template; it is never overridden.
- **Which employee:** the one whose External ID in Employment Hero is the crew member's EIN. If there is no match, or more than one, nothing is changed there and the dialog says so.
- **Before you apply:** the dialog shows the Employment Hero record's current and new values. Employment Hero is updated straight after SmartStaff; untick "Also update Employment Hero" to leave it alone.
- **If Employment Hero can't be updated** (terminated, not casual, on another template, two Level tags, or not reachable), SmartStaff is still changed and the result says to update Employment Hero by hand. A failed update can be retried from the result; it checks Employment Hero first, so nothing is changed twice.
- "Apply from a specified date" in Employment Hero is not used, because the rate comes from the template.
- Employment Hero updates are being switched on gradually. If they are off for someone, the dialog says so and asks you to update Employment Hero by hand.
