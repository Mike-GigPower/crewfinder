# 5.47.0

*Released 16 September 2026*

A new Crew Reliability Red Zone tile on Today → Operations shows who is not
turning up, and who is turning up late.

The badge counts no-shows and late arrivals across the last 30 days. The two
donuts describe the twelve-month register behind it — how bad each person's
record is, and whether they are a punctuality problem, a reliability problem, or
both.

Open the tile for the register itself: every crew member with at least 20 shifts
in the last twelve months and an incident rate of 5% or more, worst first. Each
row carries their shifts, no-shows, lates, rate, and the date of their last
incident.

A month-by-month strip across the top shows whether this is getting better or
worse over the year. The list defaults to crew with an incident in the last 90
days, and one button widens it to the full twelve months.

Lates come from the crew boss's Late flag on the call times, and no-shows from
the call's crew status. Crew we stood down ourselves are never counted against
anyone.
