# 5.71.0

*Released 9 October 2026*

All Bookings now has a Calendar view, by month or by week. Switch between **List** and **Calendar** at the top of the tab. THE GOAT remembers which one you used last.

Each booking is one bar, running from its first call to its last, so a three-day show reads as one bar across three days. Gold bars are Active bookings and grey bars are Closed. A bar with a dashed end carries on into the next or previous week. A booking whose calls have all been cancelled shows as a struck-through bar on its booking date.

**Month** shows up to four bookings a day. Click **+N more** to open that week. **Week** also lists every call under its day, with its start time and how many crew are confirmed against how many are needed. The count is red when a call is short.

Click a bar to open the booking, or a call to open the call. The search box and the Active filter work in Calendar too. The 30d, 60d and 90d buttons stay with the List.
