# 5.49.0

*Released 16 September 2026*

Shadowlands now says WHY a crew member can't be reached, not just that they can't.

A third donut, "Why", splits the lane three ways:

- Had push, lost it — they turned notifications on and the subscription has
  since died. Their phone still lists us as allowed to send, which is exactly
  why they'll tell you notifications are on. Don't open with "you never turned
  them on" — they did.
- Uses the Hub, no push — they answer their own shift offers in the Crew Hub
  but have never enabled notifications. One tap on Profile → Notifications.
- Never heard from — no sign they have ever used the Crew Hub at all.

On production at release: 4, 19 and 21 of 44.

The segments filter the drill-down like the other two donuts, and every row
carries its reason as a pill beside the At stake label.

The coverage strip no longer says crew "were never told we asked" — that was
untrue of anyone whose notifications had lapsed rather than never existed.

Needs the Crew Hub change deployed 16 September. If the Hub can't answer, the
Why donut is simply absent rather than guessed at.
