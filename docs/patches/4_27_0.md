# 4.27.0

*Released 7 August 2026*

Admins can now grant individual crew members an exemption from the direct
SmartStaff login block, with an expiry of 14, 30 or 90 days, or permanent.
Exemptions are managed from Administration → Access → Direct SmartStaff Login.
Expired exemptions stay visible as a record of who was granted access and when,
and can be re-granted in one click.

Also fixes the Administration tab not scrolling, which made the Access section
unreachable at normal zoom.
