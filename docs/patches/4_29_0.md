# 4.29.0

*Released 8 August 2026*

Ask the GOAT can now answer questions about recruitment.

Numbers. How we're tracking against the intake target, the funnel from applied through to crew, and how long each stage is taking. Ask in plain English — "how are we tracking against the target?" or "what's our no-show rate?"

What needs chasing. A count of candidates flagged across the piles, broken down by reason — aged, contract unsigned, EH incomplete, session passed, and the rest.

Notes
It reports counts and figures only. It won't name candidates — for that, use the Recruitment tab, which shows exactly the same flags per person.
Every figure is timestamped in Melbourne local time, so you can tell whether the tab needs a refresh.
Some figures carry a caveat and the GOAT will pass it on. Registration rate in particular is reading low because it depends on the Booked status, which the TryBooking import fills in — until that runs for a real session, treat it as provisional.
Recruitment figures are admin-only.
