# 5.93.0

*Released 6 November 2026*

Changing a crew member from **Declined** to **Unconfirmed** in a call's Booked Crew list now asks whether to send them a new offer.

**Send offer** re-opens the call in Crew Hub and sends the usual offer notification. If they declined linked calls together (for example a load-in and its load out), all of them re-open as one offer, and the prompt lists each call with its date.

**Change without sending** changes just that one row, as before, with no notification.

The prompt warns when the call is full (if they accept, they'll go on standby), and won't send an offer for a call that has already started or been cancelled.
