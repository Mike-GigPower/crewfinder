# 5.80.1

*Released 22 October 2026*

The Call and Booking dialogs now show the SmartStaff Notes field on their Details tab, separate from Crew Notes.

- **Call dialog:** a Notes row after Crew shows the call's notes, for example "8x LX, 8xVX".
- **Booking dialog:** the Notes row is always shown, with a dash when the booking has none, instead of disappearing.
- Line breaks in notes are kept, and characters such as & show as typed.
