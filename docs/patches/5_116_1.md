# 5.116.1

*Released 20 November 2026*

Every panel on Today → System now explains itself. A small ⓘ sits after each heading: hover it for a plain-English explanation, or click it to pin the explanation open until you click away or press Esc.

A status legend sits under the toolbar — green normal, amber slower than usual, red slow or failing, grey no requests yet — and a "What am I looking at?" link opens a new System tab guide in Docs Centre, which also sets out what to do when THE GOAT feels slow.

Help text and documentation only. Nothing about the measurements, thresholds, polling or the log has changed.
